Our Special Moments

Every story has a beginning, and ours started with something so simple that neither of us could've guessed where it'd lead.If someone had told me back then that one day you'd become such an important part of my life, I probably would've laughed.What started as random conversations slowly turned into something I started looking forward to every single day. One conversation became hours of talking. Those "I'll sleep early today" promises somehow always turned into our 11 PM calls that lasted until neither of us could keep our eyes open. College became so much more than just lectures and assignments because you were there.From walking around campus for absolutely no reason, sitting together during classes, waiting for each other between lectures, and finding excuses to spend "just five more minutes" together... every ordinary day quietly became another memory.Then came the foosball matches where we both acted like it was the World Cup final, the basketball games where I'd watch you play while secretly cheering for you, the random canteen visits, the endless photos, the teasing, the small fights, the "I'm not talking to you" moments that never lasted very long, and the laughter that somehow made everything okay again.Without even realizing it, we created a world that was completely ours.A world filled with inside jokes that nobody else understands, random selfies that somehow became favorites, little celebrations, comforting silences, stupid conversations, long walks, and moments that never seemed important until they became memories.Looking back now, I don't think our story was ever made up of grand gestures.It was built one ordinary day at a time.One late-night call.One basketball game.One foosball match.One random picture.One laugh.One hug.One memory after another.And before I knew it...You had become my favorite part of every day.Today, when I look through these photos, I don't just see pictures.I see our journey.Every smile, every laugh, every little moment that slowly turned into the most beautiful chapter of my life.And if these memories are only the beginning, then I honestly can't wait to see everything that's still waiting for us. ❤️

That Stolen Kiss
I still laugh whenever I see this one.Out of all the pictures we clicked that night, this is the one that somehow became my favorite.There was so much happening around us—the music was loud, everyone was busy enjoying themselves, and somehow you still managed to steal my entire attention... and apparently a kiss too.I don't even remember what song was playing anymore.But I do remember how happy I felt in that moment.I wish I could bottle up that feeling and keep it forever.Because if happiness had a picture, it'd probably look a lot like this one.
Five Minutes That Felt Longer
I don't know what we were talking about here.Maybe something serious.Maybe something completely stupid.But looking at this picture now, it doesn't even matter.I love how we're not looking at the camera.We're just looking at each other.Like the rest of the world didn't exist for those few minutes.It's such a simple picture, but it reminds me that my favorite conversations have never needed perfect places.An auto ride.A random road.You.Apparently that's all it takes to make one of my favorite memories. ✨
The People Who Accidentally Became Home
This picture makes me smile for so many reasons.Not because everyone is looking perfect.Not because the lighting is amazing.But because it reminds me of one of those college days that felt completely normal while we were living it.Now I look back and realize those were the days we'll miss the most.Everyone trying to fit into one frame.Someone definitely saying "wait, one more."Someone blinking.Someone laughing before the photo was even clicked.Complete chaos.And somehow...those are always the pictures that end up meaning the most.I'm really glad we have this one.🎂💖
